Solara Bay is an original 3D open-world crime game that runs offline in a desktop browser from static files. Courier Dario Voss owes fixer Mara Quinn one last job — "The Solara Job", six missions across five districts (Marina, Downtown, Old Town, Harbor, The Hills): a pickup, stealing the gold Sunray GT sportscar, three timed taxi fares, a Harbor shootout, a wanted-level-4 getaway, and the Marina heist. The city is explorable on foot and by car, with 14 traffic cars, 24 pedestrians, parked cars to steal, a wanted level of 0–5 with chasing police cars and on-foot cops who shoot and bust, punch and pistol combat, 30 Sun Coins, health/armour/ammo pickups, a HUD with cash, health, stars, ammo, clock and a live minimap, title/pause/busted/wasted/complete screens, and a localStorage save that autosaves every 15 seconds. Sound is fully procedural WebAudio: engine hum, gunshots, a two-tone police siren and a synthwave music loop. The 3D engine is hand-written vanilla WebGL — matrices, shaders and box geometry written for this game, no library vendored — and every name, character, place, vehicle and song is original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move/drive, Shift to run, mouse drag to orbit the camera, E to steal a car or interact, Space to punch (or handbrake while driving), F or click to fire the pistol, R to reload, P to pause.
