HOW TO PLAY

  • W A S D — walk / drive   Shift — sprint   Q / E-drag — camera
  • E — steal / enter / exit a car   F — punch   Click / J — shoot
  • 1 / 2 — fists / pistol   R — reload   H — horn   M — mute
  • P / Esc — pause   Follow the gold beacon and the minimap marker for each job.
  • Stars mean the Solmar Bay police are hunting you. Break line of sight to cool off.
  • Grab the 20 gold sun coins hidden around the city — each pays $25.
